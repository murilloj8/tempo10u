Refs & Rules

Read the Ref

Every whistle comes with a hand signal. If you know the signals, you'll always know what happened — and what to do next.

Fouls vs. violations

Foul = illegal CONTACT

Pushing, holding, hitting arms, running into a set defender. The ref first raises a closed fist.

What happens: the other team gets the ball, or free throws if the player was shooting or the team is in the bonus. Fouls count against the player (5 = foul out) and the team.

Violation = breaking a RULE

Traveling, double dribble, 3 seconds, stepping out of bounds. The ref first raises an open hand.

What happens: the other team gets the ball out of bounds. No free throws, and it doesn't count as a foul.

The basic rules of basketball

The teams

Five players per team on the court. The goal: score in your basket and stop the other team from scoring in theirs.

Scoring

2 points for a basket inside the 3-point line.
3 points for a basket from behind the 3-point line.
1 point for each made free throw.

Starting the game

A jump ball in the center circle starts the game. After that, the possession arrow decides who gets the ball on held balls.

Moving with the ball

You must dribble to move with the ball. When you stop, keep one foot (your pivot foot) on the floor. You can pivot all you want!

One dribble at a time

Dribble with one hand at a time. Once you stop dribbling and hold the ball, you can't start dribbling again — pass or shoot.

Out of bounds

The lines are out. If the ball or the player holding it touches the line or the floor outside it, the other team gets the ball.

3 seconds

On offense, you can't stay in the paint for more than 3 seconds in a row.

Backcourt (over and back)

Once your team brings the ball across half court, you can't take it back into the backcourt.

5 seconds

Inbounding: you have 5 seconds to pass the ball in. Holding the ball with a defender guarding you closely: pass, shoot or dribble within 5 seconds.

Free throws

Fouled while shooting? You shoot free throws: 2 for a 2-point try, 3 for a 3-point try. If your shot went in anyway, it counts and you get 1 free throw — an "and-one"!

Fouling out

A player who gets 5 fouls must leave the game. Play smart defense with your feet!

Sportsmanship

Shake hands, respect the refs, and help opponents up. Arguing can get a technical foul.

Our league: SBMSA Junior Girls — National League

Our games are played under high-school (NFHS) rules, with these special SBMSA rules for the Junior League (ages 9–10). Tempo plays in the National League, so the Junior League rules below apply to us (the American League has its own, different rules).

TopicOur league rule
Basket & ball10-foot goal. 28.5" ball (size 6).
Free-throw line13 feet for Junior League girls (closer than the 15-foot high-school line).
Game length4 quarters of 12 minutes with a running clock. The clock stops for timeouts, free-throw lineups and technical fouls. In the second half of the 4th quarter it also stops for all free throws, and in the last minute of the 2nd and 4th quarters it stops on every whistle.
Breaks2 minutes between quarters, 5 minutes at halftime. Be ready on time — a team that isn't ready gets a technical foul.
Timeouts4 per team per game (no more than 2 in each half), 1 minute each.
OvertimeRegular season: one 2-minute overtime, then the game can end in a tie. Playoffs: keep playing 2-minute overtimes until someone wins.
Playing timeEveryone plays! 1st quarter: the starting five play the whole quarter. 2nd quarter: the other five play. 3rd quarter: split into two halves with different groups. 4th quarter: free substitution. Every player plays at least 1.5 quarters.
DefenseMan-to-man only — no zone defense. Switching is allowed any time. You may only double-team the player WITH the ball (always allowed inside the paint) — no "chaser" defense. Inside the 3-point line, guard your player from a reasonable distance (the ref decides).
Inbounds playsThe defense must set up man-to-man before the ball is thrown in.
No backcourt defenseYou can't guard the ball handler in her backcourt (no full-court press). Exception: in the last 60 seconds of the game, a team that is NOT ahead by 10 or more may defend the backcourt.
OffenseNo "isolation" plays where everyone clears out for a one-on-one. Share the ball — PASS FIRST!
Breaking a special rule(Zone, backcourt defense, isolation) First time = warning. After that = technical foul.
Fouls5 fouls and a player is out of the game. A team shoots 2 free throws starting with the other team's 5th team foul of each quarter.
Technical fouls2 free throws plus the ball for the other team. Two conduct technicals = ejection.
Big leadIf a team is ahead by 20 or more in the second half of the 4th quarter, the clock keeps running.
UniformLeague jersey and shorts at every game, jersey tucked in, gym shoes. Undershirts must all be the same solid color.
