Rules of the Game

Learn the basic rules of basketball, then the special rules of our league: SBMSA Junior Girls, National League. One rule at a time. When you've seen every rule in a group today, its button turns green.

Coach note: Our league rules come from the SBMSA 2024–25 rule book for the Junior League (National League). The book lists the free-throw distance as "13 feet for Junior League girls and 14 feet for National League" — Coach will confirm which line we use and update anything that changes in the 2026–27 rule book.

Sources: SBMSA Basketball Rules 2024–2025 · SBMSA Basketball · High-school rules: NFHS.
