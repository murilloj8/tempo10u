Referee signals

Read the Ref

  • Every whistle comes with a hand signal.
  • Learn them one at a time.
  • See every signal in a group today and its button turns green.

Foul = illegal CONTACT

  • Pushing, holding, hitting arms, running into a defender.
  • The ref raises a closed fist.

Violation = breaking a RULE

  • Traveling, double dribble, 3 seconds, stepping out.
  • The ref raises an open hand.
