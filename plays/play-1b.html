Play 1B · Screen the Screener

Option when #3 is covered · #1 screens for #5, they swap spots, and the ball swings to reset.

Step 0 of 8

Step-by-step: tap Next step to run one action, then talk it through before moving on.

Speed

The action

  1. Set up. Two-guard front. #1 has the ball on the right.
  2. 1. #1 passes across to #4. Same time: #5 sets a screen on #3’s defender.
  3. 2. #3 cuts off #5’s screen and across the lane.
  4. 3. #5 fades out to the right wing.
  5. 4. No pass: X3 fights through and denies. #3 stays on the left block.
  6. 5. #1 leaves the top and sets a screen for #5 on the right side.
  7. 6. #5 cuts off #1’s screen up to the top — #1’s old spot.
  8. 7. #1 fades out to the right wing — #5’s old spot.
  9. 8. #4 swings the ball to #5 up top. The floor is reset — run it again.

Key

Tempo player Defender (man-to-man) Cut / move Pass Screen

Coaching points

Read the defense: if #3 is denied, don’t force the pass — #4 holds it and stays patient. #1: set a still, wide screen. #5: wait for it, then cut tight off #1’s shoulder to the top. Swapping spots keeps the floor spaced, and the ball swing makes the defense shift again — patience creates the next open look.
